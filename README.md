## 🐈 Don't Touch The Cats

This site now has a minigame. Open `index.html` (no build step, no dependencies) and keep your
cursor away from the floating SVG cats. Touch one and the whole page explodes. Cats get faster
and multiply the longer you survive, and your best time is saved locally.

On touch devices, drag your finger to play.
